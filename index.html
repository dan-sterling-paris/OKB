About the Show

'OK, BOOMER' uses music and humour throughout to create a lively, informative and above all accessible piece of theatre examining not only the trials of ageing but also the myths and rivalries of the different generations in the modern world.

It is true that many of us, in our younger days, may have vaguely thought about retirement in a "cottage by the sea" kind of way, while secretly nurturing negative ideas about ageing, about what we think we are capable of as we get older. And this can seriously affect our self-esteem later. In fact, the whole thing can turn into a kind of self-fulfilling prophecy. We become what we expect to be.

So in our production, we hope to draw attention to some of these problems, and go some way to celebrating ageing in a positive and optimistic manner.

The six talented actor-musicians in the cast perform live the music of three generations with songs ranging from David Bowie to Chappell Roan, from Norah Jones to The Killers, to create an entertaining, informative and above all accessible piece of highly entertaining community theatre.

Story

In a small village somewhere in rural Scotland, Cameron is enduring a small seventieth birthday party organised by his daughter Cyndi. The birthday cards are all filled with all the usual 'oldie' jokes and taunts. But in a quiet moment Cameron remembers another card he sent to himself forty years ago, not to be opened until this very day. As he reads the card, his younger self appears on stage, and together they set out on a quest into the past.

With the unlikely assistance of a young 'Gen Z' barista, our hero manages to overcome his depression, finding a fresh approach to life in his later years, finally replacing his many regrets with a more hopeful vision of the future.

Mike James - Writer / Director

Mike James has many years of experience writing, adapting and directing for the stage and television.

As Artistic Director for the Sherman Theatre, Cardiff and then the Torch Theatre (two of Wales' leading repertory theatres) he wrote and directed dozens of plays including nine children's musicals, as well as adapting classics such as the 'History of Mr Polly', 'Tess of the D'Urbervilles' and 'Vanity Fair' for the stage.

In addition, Mike's drama 'Somewhere in England' about the BBC's move to Bangor, Wales, during the war was premiered at Theatr Clwyd in October 2014.

And his musical drama, 'Tom', based on the early life of the singer Tom Jones at his birthplace, Pontypridd, on March 1st 2015, followed by an extensive national tour.

'Jackie: the Musical', celebrating the music of the 70's and the famous teenage magazine of the time, premiered in Dundee (home of original publishers DC Thomson & Co) before touring extensively throughout the UK.
