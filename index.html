About the Show

A play about ageing, memory and the myths we tell ourselves about getting older. Six actor-musicians perform live music spanning three generations, from David Bowie to Chappell Roan, Norah Jones to The Killers.

It's funny, it's moving, and it doesn't talk down to its audience.

Story

Cameron is turning seventy. His daughter Cyndi has organised a small birthday party. The cards are full of the usual 'oldie' jokes. But in a quiet moment, Cameron finds another card, one he sent to himself forty years ago, not to be opened until today. As he reads it, his younger self appears on stage, and together they set out on a quest into the past.

Along the way, a young Gen Z barista called Summer gets drawn into the journey. What follows is warm, unexpected, and surprisingly hopeful.

The Writer

Mike James - Writer / Director

Former Artistic Director of the Sherman Theatre, Cardiff and the Torch Theatre, Milford Haven. Mike has written and directed dozens of plays across both houses, including nine children's musicals and adaptations of H.G. Wells, Thomas Hardy and Thackeray.

His drama 'Somewhere in England', about the BBC's wartime move to Bangor, premiered at Theatr Clwyd in 2014. 'Tom', a musical about the young Tom Jones, opened in Pontypridd in 2015 and toured nationally.

Creative Team

Dan Blumenau - General Manager

Dan runs Sterling Paris Ltd, a production and general management consultancy working with clients including Mamma Mia! The Party, Visceral Entertainment and Arden Entertainment.

He started out as a performer, then moved into production, casting and finance. He spent eleven years at Disneyland Paris as a Financial Analyst before returning to theatre full-time. He has been Associate Casting Director with Debbie O'Brien Casting since 2005.
